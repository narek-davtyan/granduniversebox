Version 1.3.2

Release notes · November 2026

The first release under the name Grand Universe Box.

Listening

  • Radio stations made from your own music, with DJ talk, station IDs, ads, news, weather and traffic, scheduled like real radio.
  • DJ shifts with openers and closers, intros, mids and outros over the songs, callers, requests and dedications.
  • "Our favourites" montages, horoscopes, interviews, reportage, cliffhangers across the ad break and running bits over several songs.
  • Worlds and eras: slide a station sideways to hear it in another year.
  • Explicit clips can be switched off in Settings.
  • History of the last 100 songs, exportable as CSV, TSV, JSON or text.

Making stations (Mac)

  • Station editor: songs, every kind of clip, artwork, names across stations, close and reopen.
  • Songs table with a column per kind of clip, and clear marks for what needs a look, with the fix right there.
  • Station artwork: square PNG from 512 to 4096 pixels, shown sharp on any screen.
  • Local API and connector for AI assistants (off by default, this Mac only).
