Privacy Policy

Grand Universe Box · Last updated 23 November 2026

Short version: Grand Universe Box collects no personal data. Everything happens on your device.

Your library stays on your device

Songs, clips, artwork, station files, ratings, your station order, listening history and settings are stored in your library folder and in the app's own storage. We never receive them.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads, no analytics

The app has no sign-in and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code.

Update check

About once a day the app downloads one small public file from this website to see whether your version is still supported. Like any web request, the company hosting this website (GitHub) sees your IP address and the time of the request. The app sends nothing else, and we do not receive or keep this information.

Stations from a web address

If you enter a web address to download stations, the app connects to that address to fetch the files. That site sees your IP address. We are not involved, unless it is our own example catalogue on this website, where the same applies as for the update check.

Location (optional)

If you use "Find" for your location in the app's settings, the place name you type is turned into coordinates by Apple's services. It is used only for the in-app clock and weather and stays on your device.

Local API (optional, off by default)

On the Mac, when you switch on the Local API, other programs on your own computer can read and change your library through it, using a token you control. It is never reachable from the internet.

Children

The app has no accounts and collects no data, so it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions and problems: the Issues page of this site's GitHub repository
