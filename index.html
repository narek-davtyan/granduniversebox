Grand Universe Box

Radio stations from worlds that never were — made from your own music.

Grand Universe Box turns the songs on your Mac, iPhone or iPad into radio stations. Songs are mixed with DJ talk, station IDs, ads, news, weather and traffic, and scheduled the way real radio is. Every station keeps running while you listen to another, so when you tune in you land in the middle of whatever is on air.

Your music, offline

Everything plays from files on your device. No streaming, no sign-in, no connection needed.

Real radio flow

DJ shifts, intros over the song's opening, callers, requests, dedications, ad breaks and newscasts.

Worlds and eras

Stations belong to fictional places and years. Slide a station through its eras and hear it change.

Build your own

The station editor on the Mac creates and edits stations, and closes them into signed .gab files to share.

Get it

Coming soon to the App Store for Mac, iPhone and iPad.

Your privacy

Grand Universe Box collects no personal data. Your library, ratings and history stay on your device. The only thing the app downloads is one small file from this website, about once a day, to check whether your version is still supported. Read the Privacy Policy.
